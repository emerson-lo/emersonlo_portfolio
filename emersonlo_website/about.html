Info

I’m a product designer
based in Los Angeles, California.

My interest in design and craft started early. Growing up, I took classes in ceramics, silkscreen printing, photography and video editing at ArtCenter College of Design and the Armory Center for the Arts in Pasadena. That’s where I first began exploring art, and where I fell in love with making things by hand.

At UC Davis, I kept following that pull toward creativity and design, and found something new alongside it: a fascination with how people think, perceive and interact with the things around them, especially in digital spaces. As technology keeps evolving, I want to build intuitive products that are both functional and beautiful, that improve people’s lives, and that make the time we spend with screens feel more humane.

Design Philosophy

Cultivated Intuition

I believe that strong design intuition is developed through a diverse, interdisciplinary exploration of the world around us. Traveling, absorbing new environments, and experiencing different cultures profoundly shape how the human brain perceives, understands, and ultimately produces design. Beyond screens and interfaces, observing how things look, feel, and function in the physical world helps me develop a deeper cognitive intuition for what feels right. I’m not afraid of trying new things, always strive toward being ‘best-in-class’, and always strive to be curious about the unknown.

Intelligent Interfaces

The future of personal computing is astounding to me as our interfaces seamlessly blend with and understand our daily intentions. As artificial intelligence advances, I believe it will fundamentally elevate technology from a static tool into a proactive, adaptive partner. This shift transforms design by allowing us to craft highly personalized experiences that anticipate user needs rather than just reacting to them. Ultimately, I am curious about how AI can help us interact more fluidly with the physical world, making complex technology feel completely natural and invisible.
